Vice Beach Roads

A community reconstruction of Vice Beach roads and lanes, based on the Yanis map.

Explore and report

Drag to pan and scroll to zoom. Click the map to select a location, then copy its coordinates or a link to include in an issue. Use Copy issue details to prepare a report.

Coordinates

The map uses a 15,000 × 20,000 canvas. Its upper-left corner is X=0, Z=0. X increases east and Z increases south. OpenDRIVE uses x=X and y=−Z.

Driving routes

Select a start and destination on driving lanes. Routes follow mapped lane directions and connections. A missing route may indicate an incomplete connection; reports and references help improve the map.

Sources

Base map: Yanis. Road cartography: ViceMineMap2 contributors. Lane layouts are community interpretations and can differ from the game. Road elevations are schematic.
